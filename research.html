Research & Technical Interests

My research and professional interests focus on enterprise AI, intelligent and connected systems, secure cloud-native architectures, IoT, and emerging technologies for smart mobility. My current research explores the use of Ultra-Wideband (UWB) communication and IoT-enabled architectures to improve vehicle safety and situational awareness. This work examines secure proximity detection, real-time vehicle-to-vehicle information exchange, edge intelligence, and cloud integration to help vehicles identify surrounding risks and respond more effectively in complex driving environments.
